Glovebox is a local, on-device car maintenance log. It requires no account. Your vehicle and service data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to us.

The free tier includes one vehicle, unlimited service records, reminders, and CSV export at no cost, always. Pro is an optional, auto-renewing subscription that removes the one-vehicle limit and adds custom service intervals. Pro is purchased and billed through your Apple App Store account.
